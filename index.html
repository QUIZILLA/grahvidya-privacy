Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Grah Vidya mobile application ("the app", "we", "us") handles, and how. We designed the app to keep your personal astrological data on your own device wherever possible.

1. Information you enter

To calculate a birth chart (Kundali), the app lets you enter details such as name, date of birth, time of birth, and birthplace. These details are stored locally on your device (in the app's local storage). We do not operate accounts and we do not upload your saved profiles to our servers.

2. How your birth data is used

Your birth details are used only to compute your chart, divisional charts, planetary periods (dashas), transits, and related astrological analysis. All of this calculation happens on your device.

3. AI-assisted answers

The app offers optional AI guidance ("AI Jyotish Scholar" and chart Q&A) powered by Google's Gemini API. When you choose to ask the AI a question:

  • Only the derived astrological details needed to interpret your question (for example: ascendant, planetary positions and dignities, divisional-chart summaries, and dasha periods) are sent to the AI service.
  • By default, your raw birth identity — name, exact date of birth, birth time, birthplace and coordinates — is not sent to the AI service.
  • For time-sensitive "when" questions, the AI may consult public web search results to provide current dates; no personal birth data is placed into those searches.

Data sent to Google's Gemini API is processed under Google's terms and privacy policy.

4. Bring-your-own-key (optional)

You may link your own Google Gemini API key so AI requests run on your own account. If you do, that key is stored only on your device and is used solely to authenticate your AI requests. We do not store your key on our servers or in logs.

5. Location / geocoding

When you search for a birthplace, the text you type may be sent to a public geocoding service (OpenStreetMap Nominatim) to convert a place name into coordinates. Only the place text you enter is sent for this lookup.

6. Data we do not collect

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not require sign-in or create user accounts.
  • We do not track your device location in the background.

7. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Your choices

You can delete any saved profile inside the app at any time, and you can remove a linked API key from the app's settings. Clearing the app's data from your device removes locally stored information.

9. Disclaimer

Grah Vidya provides astrological guidance for informational and spiritual purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: contact@grahvidya.org
